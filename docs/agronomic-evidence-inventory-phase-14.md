## 3. Claim-to-evidence boundary

The review target is the application’s own claims: crop/season availability, broad-zone fit and ranking, altitude/elevation handling, qualitative water need, seasonal timing, seed/resource estimates, duration/harvest window, yield ranges, and crop-specific tips. Their current source is the internal knowledge pack and derived plan behavior, not NISR inference or an approved external agronomic rule.

NISR data can describe recorded historical area and production within its stated aggregation and period. A high historical production value does not establish causal yield, crop suitability, profitability, future performance, or a recommendation for a specific farmer. The dataset’s missing plot conditions and its aggregate design preclude those conclusions.

Weather forecasts and service descriptions have different meaning from observed weather and crop response. They must not be converted into an automatic agronomic rule without a sourced, scoped, reviewed relationship and validation evidence. The empty `utils/weather_alert_rules.py` registry remains outside this crop-guidance inventory.
