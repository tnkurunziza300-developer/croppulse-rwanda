# CropPulse Rwanda controlled-pilot evaluation

## Purpose

Check whether a source-labelled crop plan is understandable and useful to farmers and extension advisors. The pilot evaluates usability and service quality first; it does not assume a yield or income impact.

## Pilot setup

- Work with one willing local cooperative, extension office, or research partner and obtain written agreement before collecting farmer information.
- Select one or two crops and a small set of sites only after an agronomist verifies the relevant crop content and calendar.
- Recruit a diverse group, including women farmers and people with basic phones or limited connectivity. Record consent separately from farm records.
- Use CropPulse as a decision-support aid alongside ordinary extension advice. Do not make CropPulse the only source of urgent pest, disease, or weather guidance.
- Identify a data steward, retention period, access rules, deletion process, and incident contact before field collection. The current frontend keeps entries only in the active session and is not a durable or confidential field data system.

## Measures

| Measure | Method | Report separately as |
|---|---|---|
| Plan completion | Observe users choosing district, crop, area, and planting date; record completion and time. | Usability, not impact. |
| Understanding | Ask users to explain the recommended actions, source, uncertainty, and limitations in their own words. | Comprehension. |
| Trust calibration | Ask which information they trust, what they would verify, and whether they mistake a forecast or estimate for a guarantee. | Appropriate trust, not satisfaction alone. |
| Adoption | Follow up on selected activities and record whether each was done, changed, or skipped and why. | Self-reported adoption. |
| Correction rate | Have qualified reviewers log incorrect, unclear, or unsafe guidance and time to correction. | Content quality and safety. |
| Connectivity | Record load failures, data use where measurable, and ability to complete the task under weak connectivity. | Access/reliability. |
| Farm outcomes | If partner and farmer consent permit, collect comparable baseline and endline harvest/cost measures with units and verification status. | Exploratory association unless a defensible comparison design supports causal inference. |

## Reporting rules

- Publish the number recruited, completed, followed up, and lost to follow-up; describe site and crop coverage.
- Separate product usage, satisfaction, recommendation adoption, and measured farm outcomes.
- Disclose missing data, data collection method, comparison group (if any), and uncertainty.
- Do not claim CropPulse caused a change in yield, losses, or income from a small uncontrolled pilot.
- Pause affected guidance if a qualified reviewer finds a safety issue; document the version and correction.
