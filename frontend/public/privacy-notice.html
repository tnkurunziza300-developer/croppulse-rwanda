CropPulse Rwanda account data notice

Prototype notice · version phase1-2026-10-weather-storage

Creating an account stores your email address, display name, password hash, account timestamps, and the farms, crop plans, and activities you submit in the database configured by the CropPulse operator. Passwords are not stored as plain text.

When you request a weather forecast and confirm saving it, CropPulse stores the coordinates you supplied, the provider forecast response, its retrieval time, and whether it was stale. These records are linked to your account and returned only to that signed-in account. The latest forecast for each requested coordinate is retained; deleting your account also deletes these forecast records.

This information is used to authenticate your account and provide private farm-record workflows. Farm reports remain farmer reported unless a reviewer records a separate review decision. Email verification and password recovery depend on the server email configuration. Self-service account deletion is available in account settings; minimized audit records may remain after deletion. Retention arrangements for other account records have not been finalized.

This prototype has not completed legal/privacy review or a public deployment review. Do not submit confidential or highly sensitive personal or farm information. Ask the CropPulse operator how to request account assistance or deletion before creating an account.

By checking the acceptance box, you acknowledge this notice and understand the prototype limitations described here.
